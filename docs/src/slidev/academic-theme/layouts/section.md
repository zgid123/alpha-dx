# Section Layout (`section`)

The `section` layout serves as a visual chapter or section divider in multi-part academic presentations.

## Purpose

Centered section divider for marking chapter transitions in academic presentations.

**When to use**: Between major sections of your lecture or defense — "Methodology", "Results", "Discussion". Keeps the audience oriented in long presentations.

## Usage

In your slide markdown frontmatter, set `layout: section`:

```md
---
layout: section
---

# Part II: Empirical Evaluation
## Benchmark Results & Ablation Studies
```

---

## Behavior

- Centers large heading typography with subtle academic styling.
- Establishes a distinct visual boundary between major phases or topics in long presentations.
